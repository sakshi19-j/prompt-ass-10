Interview tips

Quick reminders to help you show up prepared, thoughtful, and ready to communicate.

  • Keep your resume to one focused page and lead with outcomes, not just responsibilities.
  • Quantify projects with users, performance gains, scale, or time saved wherever possible.
  • Know every line well enough to explain the decision behind it.
  • Carry a clean PDF copy and check that links work before the interview.

  • Clarify constraints before coding and talk through your approach as it develops.
  • Start with a correct solution, then improve complexity and explain the trade-off.
  • Use small examples to test edge cases before you run out of time.
  • When stuck, state what you know and ask a precise question rather than going silent.

  • Prepare a concise introduction that connects your interests to the role.
  • Answer with specific situations, actions, and results instead of general claims.
  • Be honest about gaps and explain how you are actively closing them.
  • Bring two thoughtful questions about the team, learning, or success in the role.

  • Scan the section and secure quick wins before spending time on a hard question.
  • Estimate when possible and keep a rough time budget for each question.
  • Write down units and reread wording: many mistakes come from skipped details.
  • Practice mental math and common ratios so the basics feel automatic.

  • Enter early with a clear point, then make room for others to contribute.
  • Build on ideas by naming the useful part before adding your perspective.
  • Use examples and keep your points brief enough for the group to move forward.
  • Help the group reach a conclusion by summarizing the strongest shared ideas.
